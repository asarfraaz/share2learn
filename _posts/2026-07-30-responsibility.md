When people are asked, "What is your responsibility towards your children". We often here the following answers :
1. Marry them with a good partner in a good household
2. Give them a good education
3. Teach them good manners

And usually in that order. I will leave it upto you to decide what could be a better order

More often than not, it is the first answer which always gets the highest priority and in effect that highest focus
The rest get a secondary effort, if at all.

What is more important to realise is that, most are clear about being reposnsible for some future event or behaviour of their children

Though, if you think deeply, you will realise that very little of what their children will end up in those 3 categories depends on the parents. 
Most of the results are based on the available options present at the time. And also the opportunities that are made use of at that time. And above all, it is predestiny that decides the end result.
Yet, we put in a lot of effort, thinking and planning into these. So that we end up fulfilling our responsiblities to the best of our ability

Children are a blessing given to us
And as parents, we are responsible for their upbringing
And rightfully, most take this responsiblity quite seriously.

Well, its not children that are blessings that are given to us
Everything else given to us is also a blessing
Be it a job, spouse, house, vehicle, health, wealth, knowledge, strength, etc

And in the same way as we are responsible for children, we are responsible for these other blessings as well

We will be held accountable for them
What did we do with those blessings?
How did we earn them? ( money )
Where did we spend them? ( time, money, skills, strengths )
How did we apply them to our lives ? ( knowledge )
