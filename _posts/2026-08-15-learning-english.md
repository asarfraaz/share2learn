- Many students come from a venacular background
- They did have their schooling in English medium
- Most think in their own mother tongue and then convert it into English before
  speaking
- Nothing wrong in it, except that it takes more time
- And can give strange signals when done in a conversation, especially like an
  interview
- A delay in an answer, might be percieved as not being confident

- What they need is to learn "Spoken English"
- They know English.
- But, just knowing English words is not called learning English
- They should be able to form gramatically correct sentences. Most fail at this
- And they should learn to become fluent in "speaking" English. Not giving some extra delay in replying to questions
- They should also learn to answer to the point. Don't give generic answers
  when asked a specific question. This is more about understanding the question
  and being confident in giving the answer rather than anything to do with
  speaking in English
- One more important skill is to have good English Comprehension. Read a story
  and tell me its gist in your own words. Most fail at this. This stops them
  from thinking at a higher level of abstraction. Can lead to problems with
  strategic thinking
