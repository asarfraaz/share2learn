## Part 1: Choosing to Choose

- 4000 weeks : Our ( average ) lifespan ( when brought to a round number )

- "Brevity of life" has been the defining problem of human existence, according to many philosophers from ancient Greece to present days

- We are given 2 things ( blessed to be more precise )
    - Mental ability to make innumerable ambitious plans
    - A limited amount of time ( to act on those plans )

- Time management should therefore be everybody's chief concern
    - Time management is all life is

- The world around us, is bursting with wonder
- Are our "productivity" plans helping us experience all these wonders?
